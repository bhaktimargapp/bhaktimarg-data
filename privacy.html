Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Our Mission: Seva

Bhakti Marg is offered as a selfless digital Seva to devotees globally. To ensure this platform remains universally accessible, we operate on a strict Zero-Cost Infrastructure. Your spiritual journey is exclusively yours.

1. 100% Local Storage

Your daily Japs, sacred notes, and milestones are saved strictly within your device's internal memory. We do not operate external databases, meaning we have absolutely zero access to your personal data.

2. Google Drive Backups (Cloud Sync)

If you choose to enable Cloud Sync, the app creates a secure backup file in a hidden, app-specific folder inside your personal Google Drive. The app only requests permission to access this specific file. We cannot read your emails, view your photos, or access any other files in your Drive.

3. Your Right to Deletion

You have full control over your data. By navigating to your Profile and selecting "Delete All Data", the app will instantly erase all local records and permanently remove the backup file from your Google Drive.

4. Third-Party Services (Ads)

To keep this application free and universally accessible, we utilize Google AdMob. AdMob may collect standard device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID) to serve non-intrusive advertisements. No personal information or names entered in the app are shared with advertisers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at bhaktimargapp@gmail.com.
